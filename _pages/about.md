### Hello, world! Welcome to my website!

I am currently a Member of Technical Staff, Researcher at [OpenAI](https://openai.com/), specializing in
LLM post-training, reinforcement learning, and agent research. Previously,
I was a Senior Machine Learning Engineer at [Meta](https://engineering.fb.com/), building and scaling next-generation AI systems. 
I earned my B.S. in Computer Science from [Stanford University](https://www.stanford.edu/) with Highest Distinction and engaged in cutting-edge ML research with [Stanford AI Lab](https://ai.stanford.edu/).

Beyond engineering, I have a deep passion for mathematics. I won an [IMO Medal](https://www.imo-official.org/) in 2016 after securing the #1 Rank in the Vietnam Mathematical
Olympiad. I was fortunate to be endorsed by Fields Medalist Prof. Ngo
Bao Chau for my mathematical talent and research potential.

My ambition is to develop AGI capable of unlocking profound innovations
and creating lasting benefits for humanity.
